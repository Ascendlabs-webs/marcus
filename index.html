A Sacred Legacy of Pure Hospitality

At Markaz, every celebration is viewed as an act of noble reverence. Born from an uncompromising dedication to ancient Nawabi kitchens and southern coastal dawat traditions, we believe hospitality (*Mehmaan-nawazi*) is an art form rooted in absolute culinary sincerity.

We never cut corners with pre-packaged gravies, synthetic food colouring, or ambiguous meat provenance. Every degh of Zafrani biryani is sealed with fresh wholewheat dough and slow-simmered on smoldering wood embers, guaranteeing an aroma that announces celebration the moment your guests step into the hall.
