Our Story

Hospitality Is Our First Language

Markaz Catering began around a single family kitchen table, cooking for relatives' weddings and milad gatherings. Today the same recipes, the same generous portions and the same attentiveness to every guest travel with us to banquet halls, gardens and boardrooms across the city.

Every dish leaves our kitchen 100% halal, cooked in small batches so biryani, kebabs and curries reach your table tasting like they were made for your family alone — because they were.

What Comes to Your Table

A short taste of the spread — every menu is built around your guests, your occasion and your budget.

Dum Biryani & Pulao

Slow-cooked, layered rice in mutton, chicken or vegetable, sealed and served straight from the handi.

Kebabs & Live Grill

Seekh, boti and tikka off the charcoal, carved to order in front of your guests.

Curries & Handi

Rich gravies simmered the traditional way — family recipes, never shortcuts.

Chaat & Live Counters

Tangy, spiced street-style bites, plated fresh in front of your guests, never pre-made.

Desserts & Kheer

Chai & Sherbet Bar
