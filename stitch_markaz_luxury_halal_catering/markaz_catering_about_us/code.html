Our Royal Lineage

The Markaz Story

Sovereign Gastronomy Born Out of Unyielding Faith & Family Heritage

Markaz Catering took root over twelve years ago in the historic corridors of Thousand Lights, Chennai. Conceived by master culinary custodians, our mission was simple yet radical: restore the regal grandeur of classical Awadhi, Hyderabadi, and Mughlai culinary lineage while preserving absolute integrity in Halal slaughter, sourcing, and preparation.

In an era where banquet food often surrendered to factory-made bases and shortcuts, Markaz revived centuries-old rituals: whole spices crushed on flat stone silbattas, slow wood-fired degs sealed tightly with wheat dough, and bespoke saffron infusions sourced from Kashmiri valleys. Each dawat we cater reflects deep reverence for the sacred trust our hosts place upon our shoulders.
