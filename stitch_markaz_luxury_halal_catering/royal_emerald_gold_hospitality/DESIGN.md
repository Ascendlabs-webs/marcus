## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sovereign heritage, warm generosity, and sacred ceremonial prestige of premier South Asian culinary celebrations. Designed for weddings, walimas, grand corporate iftars, milads, and intimate family milestones, the aesthetic avoids gaudy excess in favor of refined, high-end editorial restraint. 

The visual philosophy marries classical architectural balance with warm, tactile elegance:
- **Tone:** Regal, hospitable, immaculate, reverent, and sophisticated.
- **Design Movement:** Heritage Luxury & Editorial Modernism. It incorporates subtle Islamic arch profiles, fine-hairline metallic filigree, expansive ivory breathing room, and structured editorial grids.
- **Emotional Response:** Guests and patrons should feel the honor of hospitality (*Mehmaan-nawazi*), unyielding trust in culinary and spiritual authenticity, and the reassurance of flawless execution for their most cherished life occasions.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classic royal courtyards, deep evergreen velvet, and antique gilded craftsmanship:

- **Primary (`#0A2C1E` - Deep Emerald):** The foundational pillar representing heritage, depth, and permanence. Used for primary buttons, dominant headers, ceremonial accents, and grounding backgrounds.
- **Secondary (`#E0B36F` - Royal Gold):** Warm, polished antique gold used for primary callouts, delicate structural borders, active states, and focal icons. Accompanied by **Bright Gold (`#F1CB90`)** for gentle hover transitions and luminous highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#12402B` - Mid Green):** A supporting jewel tone for secondary hover states, elevated card surfaces in dark-tinted modules, and muted accents. Accompanied by **Darker Green (`#071F15`)** for deep atmospheric backdrops.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF6EC` - Ivory) & Warm Beige (`#F2EADA`):** The primary tactile canvas colors. They evoke hand-laid calligraphic paper, linen table settings, and warmth, eliminating the clinical coldness of pure white.
- **Text & Contrast (`#1B241D` - Ink):** A deep charcoal-green ink used for ultra-legible body typography, keeping reading experiences soft yet precise.

## Typography

Typography balances dramatic classical sovereignty with airy modern legibility:

- **Display & Headings:** Rendered in Bodoni Moda to evoke imperial invitations, royal charters, and distinguished hospitality. Titles feature intentional tracking (+0.02em to +0.05em) and spacious line heights.
- **Body & Captions:** Rendered in Montserrat with light (`300`) and regular weights to preserve an open, breathable layout that balances the high-contrast display serif.
- **Caps & Micro-copy (`label-caps`):** Formatted with strict uppercase styling and wide tracking (`+0.12em`) for ceremonial categories (e.g., *WALIMA MENU*, *CANAPÉS & SHARBAT*, *LIVE DEG STATIONS*).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors classical architectural symmetry, emphasizing generous negative space reminiscent of royal courtyards and bespoke dinner programs:

- **Grid Model:** 12-column responsive grid on desktop with a maximum content canvas of `1280px`. Breakpoints scale symmetrically:
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `3rem` lateral margins. Section dividers use delicate 1px gold hairline rules (`#E0B36F` at 30% opacity).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, `2rem` margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` margins.
- **Rhythm & Structure:** Alternating asymmetric culinary imagery flanked by centered, formal typographic compositions. Multi-course menu layouts rely on generous vertical rhythm, utilizing `space-xl` between narrative sections to prevent cognitive clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid plastic, artificial drop shadows, depth is achieved through tactile material layering, warm ambient diffusion, and gilded hairline borders:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Base Surface:* Tactile Ivory (`#FBF6EC`).
  - *Container Surface:* Warm Beige (`#F2EADA`) or crisp layered Ivory with a 1px border of `#E0B36F` at 25% opacity.
  - *Immersive Royal Surface:* Deep Emerald (`#0A2C1E`) container panels with faint gold micro-borders for banquet tiers and bespoke consultation modules.
- **Ambient Shadows:** Shadows are warm-tinted and deeply diffused:
  - *Resting Cards:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(10, 44, 30, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(27, 36, 29, 0.04)`.
  - *Floating Modals & Curated Menus:* `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(7, 31, 21, 0.15), 0 1px 0 rgba(224, 179, 111, 0.2) inset`.
- **Architectural Accents:** Subtle Islamic multi-foil and pointed arch profiles (*Ogee* / *Moorish* arch motifs) frame high-end photography without heavy 3D bevels.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a soft, architectural restraint (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Structural Components:** Interactive elements, booking modals, and input fields utilize subtle 4px (`rounded-sm` / `0.25rem`) corners. This maintains sharp, tailored precision while preventing harsh industrial edges.
- **Arch Motifs (Feature Windows):** Imagery containers, tier showcases, and ceremonial category headers may feature an Islamic arched top-edge (curved top with flat base: `border-radius: 999px 999px 4px 4px` on select hero framing elements).
- **Filigree Details:** Separators and divider accents feature centered diamond or minaret finial geometry integrated directly into thin 1px horizontal gold rules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign:** Deep Emerald (`#0A2C1E`) background, Bright Gold (`#F1CB90`) border (1px), Ivory text. Subtle scale transition on hover (`transform: translateY(-1px)`) with background shifting to Mid Green (`#12402B`).
- **Secondary Imperial:** Transparent background, Gold (`#E0B36F`) border (1px), Deep Emerald text. On hover, fills with Ivory or Gold at 10% opacity.
- **Form Factor:** Heights of 44px to 48px, 4px corner radius, tracked uppercase Montserrat label (`label-caps`).

### Cards & Tasting Menus
- **Menu Item Cards:** Ivory card surface bordered by a 1px hairline rule of `#E0B36F` (30% opacity). Includes course title in Bodoni Moda, dietary certifications (e.g., *100% HMC Hand-Slaughtered Halal*) in uppercase badges, and price/guest tiers.
- **Hero Arch Cards:** Photography-driven cards for wedding and walima curation, framed with top-arch masks and gold baseline metadata.

### Input Fields & Booking Requests
- **Fields:** Warm Beige (`#F2EADA`) background, border-bottom in Gold (`#E0B36F`, 40% opacity) or full-stroke border with 4px radius. Focus state shifts border to Gold (`#E0B36F`) with a 1px soft outer glow `rgba(224, 179, 111, 0.25)`. Text sits in Ink (`#1B241D`).
- **Date & Guest Steppers:** Bespoke counters featuring thin gold increment/decrement controls.

### Chips & Dietary Certifications
- **Status & Dietary Chips:** Pill or soft-square badges with Warm Beige background, 1px Gold border, and Deep Emerald typography. Accompanied by a discrete gold crescent/geometric seal for 100% Halal verification.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Controls:** 4px rounded checkboxes and circular radios edged with `#E0B36F`. Selected states fill with Deep Emerald (`#0A2C1E`) featuring an antique gold center tick or pip.

### Ceremonial Quote & Menu Drawer
- **Drawer / Modal:** Deep Emerald canvas with filigree headers, gold divider rules, and an Ivory guest counter panel designed for real-time quotation of Grand Receptions, Walimas, and Corporate Iftars.
